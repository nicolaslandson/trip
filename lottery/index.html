✅ How to Check Your Spanish Christmas Lottery Ticket 2025

There are several ways to check if your Lotería de Navidad ticket won a prize:

1. Official Website (Loterías y Apuestas del Estado)

  • Website: www.loteriasyapuestas.es
  • Method: Enter your 5-digit ticket number in the prize checker
  • Available: From December 22, 2025 after the draw (around 9:30 AM Spanish time)
  • Language: Website available in Spanish, English, and other languages

2. Mobile App

  • App name: "Loterías y Apuestas" (official app)
  • Available: iOS and Android
  • Features: Check results, scan ticket barcode, view prize lists

3. Where You Bought the Ticket

  • Return to the lottery retailer (estanco or lottery shop) where you purchased the ticket
  • They can check your ticket number against the official results
  • Bring your ticket or note the number

4. TV & Media

  • The draw is broadcast live on Spanish TV (RTVE) on December 22nd
  • Results published in major Spanish newspapers the next day
  • Many Spanish news websites publish full prize lists
💡 Tip: Your ticket has a 5-digit number (e.g., 12345). Check this number against the winning numbers published after the draw. Even if your exact number didn't win "El Gordo" (the big prize), you might have won a smaller prize!

🎯 2025 Results & Winning Numbers

The Spanish Christmas Lottery draw takes place on December 22, 2025 starting at 9:00 AM (Spanish time, CET).

El Gordo (The Big Prize)

  • Prize: €4,000,000 per full ticket (€200 per décimo/share)
  • How it works: The winning 5-digit number is drawn, and all tickets with that number win
  • Tradition: Winning numbers are sung by schoolchildren from San Ildefonso school in Madrid
  • Draw time: Usually announced around 9:30-10:00 AM

Other Prize Categories

  • 2nd Prize: €1,250,000 per full ticket (€125,000 per décimo)
  • 3rd Prize: €500,000 per full ticket (€50,000 per décimo)
  • 4th Prize: €200,000 per full ticket (€20,000 per décimo)
  • 5th Prize: €60,000 per full ticket (€6,000 per décimo)
  • Additional prizes: Thousands of smaller prizes for numbers ending in the same digits
⚠️ Important: Official results for 2025 will be published on December 22, 2025 after the draw. Always check the official website for accurate results.

💰 Understanding Prize Categories

The Spanish Christmas Lottery has a unique prize structure:

How Tickets Work

  • Full ticket (billete): Costs €200, contains one 5-digit number
  • Décimo (share): Costs €20, represents 1/10 of a full ticket
  • Same number: All décimos with the same number share the prize proportionally

Prize Distribution

  • If you have a full ticket (€200) with the winning number, you get the full prize
  • If you have a décimo (€20), you get 10% of the prize
  • Example: El Gordo = €4,000,000 for full ticket, €400,000 for décimo

Reimbursement Prizes

  • Even if you don't win a major prize, you might get your money back
  • Numbers ending in the same last digits as winning numbers get smaller prizes
  • Check the official prize list for all categories

📅 When is the 2025 Draw?

  • Date: December 22, 2025 (always December 22nd each year)
  • Time: Draw starts at 9:00 AM Spanish time (CET)
  • Duration: Several hours (El Gordo usually announced by 9:30-10:00 AM)
  • Location: Teatro Real (Royal Theatre) in Madrid
  • Broadcast: Live on RTVE (Spanish public television) and online

Time Zones

  • Spain (CET): 9:00 AM
  • UK (GMT): 8:00 AM
  • US East Coast (EST): 3:00 AM
  • US West Coast (PST): 12:00 AM (midnight)

🛒 How to Buy Spanish Christmas Lottery Tickets

In Spain

  • Estancos (tobacco shops): Most common place to buy tickets
  • Lottery shops: Official Loterías y Apuestas del Estado retailers
  • Online: Official website (www.loteriasyapuestas.es) - requires Spanish ID/NIE
  • Price: €20 per décimo (share), €200 for full ticket
  • When to buy: Tickets go on sale in July, but most popular numbers sell out early

From Abroad

  • Online services: Some third-party services allow international purchases (check legitimacy)
  • Friends in Spain: Ask someone in Spain to buy for you
  • Travel to Spain: Buy during a visit (tickets available until draw day)
  • Important: You must claim prizes in Spain or through official channels

Popular Numbers

  • Many people buy tickets ending in specific numbers (birthdays, anniversaries)
  • Some numbers are considered "lucky" and sell out quickly
  • Random numbers are often easier to find available

🎄 The Tradition of El Gordo

The Spanish Christmas Lottery (Lotería de Navidad) is one of the world's oldest and largest lotteries:

  • History: First draw in 1812, making it over 200 years old
  • Name "El Gordo": Means "The Fat One" - referring to the biggest prize
  • Cultural importance: Nearly 75% of Spanish adults buy tickets each year
  • Total prize pool: Over €2.5 billion distributed in prizes
  • Unique draw: Numbers are drawn by schoolchildren from San Ildefonso school, who sing the numbers in a traditional chant
  • Social event: Families gather to watch the draw on TV, checking tickets together

Why It's Special

  • Unlike other lotteries, many people win smaller prizes (not just one big winner)
  • The draw is a major cultural event, broadcast live for hours
  • Winning numbers are often shared among friends/family who bought décimos together
  • It's a Christmas tradition as important as the holiday itself for many Spaniards

💵 How to Claim Your Prize

If You Win

  • Small prizes (under €2,000): Can be claimed at any lottery retailer in Spain
  • Medium prizes (€2,000-€35,000): Claim at lottery offices (Oficinas de Loterías)
  • Large prizes (over €35,000): Must claim at main lottery office in Madrid or regional headquarters
  • Required documents: Valid ID (passport for non-residents), original ticket
  • Tax: Prizes are tax-free in Spain (unlike many other countries)

From Abroad

  • You can claim prizes as a non-resident
  • Bring passport and original ticket
  • Large prizes may require additional documentation
  • Consider consulting with a Spanish lawyer for very large prizes

Deadline

  • Prizes must be claimed within 3 months of the draw
  • After that, the prize expires
  • Keep your ticket safe - it's the only proof of ownership

📝 Tips for Checking Your Ticket

  • ✔ Write down your ticket number before the draw (in case you lose the ticket)
  • ✔ Check multiple times - the official website can be slow on draw day due to traffic
  • ✔ Don't just check for El Gordo - check all prize categories
  • ✔ If you bought a décimo, remember you get 10% of the full prize
  • ✔ Keep your ticket safe until you've confirmed results
  • ✔ Be patient - the website may crash due to millions of people checking at once
  • ✔ Use the official app for faster checking
  • ✔ Check numbers ending in the same digits - you might have a smaller prize
