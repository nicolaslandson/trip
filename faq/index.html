Travel Planning

When is the best time to visit Spain?
Spring (April-June) and fall (September-October) offer the best weather with fewer crowds. Summer (July-August) is hot and crowded, especially in southern cities. Winter is mild but some attractions have shorter hours.

How much money should I budget per day in Spain?
Budget travelers: €40-60/day. Mid-range: €70-120/day. Luxury: €150+/day. This includes accommodation, food, transport, and attractions.

Do I need to book hotels and flights in advance?
Yes, especially for popular cities and peak season (summer, Easter, festivals). Booking 2-3 months ahead usually gets better prices.

Are the partner links safe to use?
Yes. We only work with trusted partners like Booking.com, Aviasales, and major car rental companies. All links go to secure booking platforms.

Spain Travel Tips

Do I need to speak Spanish?
Not required in tourist areas, but knowing basic phrases helps a lot. English is spoken in major cities, less so in smaller towns.

Is Spain expensive?
Spain is moderately priced. Cheaper than France or Italy, more expensive than Portugal or Eastern Europe. Southern cities (Seville, Granada) are generally cheaper than Barcelona or Madrid.

What's the deal with siesta?
Many small shops close 2-5pm for siesta (afternoon rest). Restaurants, supermarkets, and tourist sites usually stay open. Dinner is typically 9-11pm, later than most countries.

Is tap water safe to drink?
Yes, in all major cities. Ask for "agua del grifo" to get free tap water in restaurants (though some may charge a small fee).

Technical Questions

Why don't my results show?
Make sure you entered a valid city name in Spain. Try using Spanish spelling (e.g., "Sevilla" instead of "Seville") or English (both work).

Can I use this for trips longer than 7 days?
The calculator is optimized for short trips (1-7 days). For longer stays, multiply the daily estimates or use the tool multiple times.

Do you have a mobile app?
No, but our website works perfectly on mobile browsers. Add it to your home screen for quick access.

Planificación de viaje

¿Cuándo es la mejor época para visitar España?
Primavera (abril-junio) y otoño (septiembre-octubre) ofrecen el mejor clima con menos turistas. Verano (julio-agosto) es caluroso y concurrido. Invierno es suave pero algunas atracciones tienen horarios reducidos.

¿Cuánto presupuesto necesito por día?
Viajero económico: €40-60/día. Rango medio: €70-120/día. Lujo: €150+/día. Incluye alojamiento, comida, transporte y atracciones.

¿Debo reservar hoteles y vuelos con antelación?
Sí, especialmente para ciudades populares y temporada alta (verano, Semana Santa, festivales). Reservar con 2-3 meses de antelación suele conseguir mejores precios.

Consejos de viaje

¿Es cara España?
España tiene precios moderados. Más barata que Francia o Italia, más cara que Portugal o Europa del Este. Las ciudades del sur (Sevilla, Granada) suelen ser más económicas que Barcelona o Madrid.

¿Es segura el agua del grifo?
Sí, en todas las ciudades principales. Pide "agua del grifo" para obtener agua gratis en restaurantes (aunque algunos pueden cobrar una pequeña cantidad).
