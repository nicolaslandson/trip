💡 Essential Travel Tips

🌤️ Best Time to Visit

Spring (April-June) and fall (September-October) offer pleasant weather and fewer crowds. Summer can be extremely hot in southern cities.

🚆 Transportation

Spain has excellent high-speed AVE trains. Book 2-3 months ahead for up to 70% discount. City metros are efficient and affordable.

🍽️ Food Culture

Lunch is 2-4pm, dinner starts at 9pm. Try the menú del día (3-course lunch) for €10-15. Best value in Spain!

💰 Money Saving

Many museums are free on Sundays. Buy combined tickets. Tap water is safe to drink. Free tapas in Granada and León!
