✈️ Flights to Spain

Find Cheap Flights to Spain

Flying to Spain is the most common way to reach the country. With major international airports in Madrid, Barcelona, and many regional airports, there are plenty of options to find affordable flights. Prices vary significantly depending on when you book, your departure city, and travel dates.

Use our flight search tool to compare prices from multiple airlines and booking sites. You can save up to 40% by booking at the right time and comparing different routes.

🛫 Major Airports in Spain

Madrid-Barajas (MAD)

Spain's largest airport and main international hub

  • Best for: Central Spain, connections to South America
  • Connections: Excellent train/bus to all major cities
  • Terminals: 4 (T1-T4)

Barcelona-El Prat (BCN)

Second largest, gateway to Catalonia and Costa Brava

  • Best for: Barcelona, Costa Brava, Pyrenees
  • Connections: Easy train/metro to city center
  • Budget airlines: Ryanair, Vueling, easyJet

Malaga (AGP)

Andalusia's main airport, Costa del Sol

  • Best for: Costa del Sol, Marbella, Ronda, Granada
  • Connections: Direct train to city center (€1.80, 12 min!)

Valencia (VLC) & Seville (SVQ)

Regional airports with good connections

  • Best for: Regional travel, avoiding Madrid connections
  • Often cheaper: Flights to smaller airports can be less expensive

💰 Average Flight Prices

From Europe

  • London → Madrid: €50-150 (seasonal)
  • Paris → Barcelona: €60-180
  • Amsterdam → Madrid: €80-200
  • Rome → Barcelona: €70-160
  • Frankfurt → Seville: €100-220

From North America

  • New York → Madrid: €400-800
  • Miami → Madrid: €350-700
  • Toronto → Barcelona: €500-900
  • Los Angeles → Madrid: €600-1000
  • Chicago → Barcelona: €550-950

Note: Prices are approximate and vary dramatically by season, booking time, and airline. Summer (June-August) is most expensive.

💡 Money-Saving Tips

  • ✔ Book early: 6-8 weeks in advance for best prices (can save 30-40%)
  • ✔ Be flexible with dates: Flying Tuesday-Thursday is typically 20-30% cheaper
  • ✔ Use comparison sites: Compare prices across multiple airlines and booking platforms
  • ✔ Consider nearby airports: Flying to nearby cities and taking train can save money
  • ✔ Travel off-season: April-May and September-October offer better prices
  • ✔ Set price alerts: Monitor prices and book when they drop
  • ✔ Bundle deals: Sometimes flight+hotel packages are cheaper
  • ✔ Clear browser cookies: Prices may increase on repeat searches
  • ✔ Check return flights separately: Sometimes two one-way tickets are cheaper
  • ✔ Sign up for airline newsletters: Get notified of flash sales and promotions

✈️ Major Airlines Serving Spain

Budget Airlines

  • Ryanair: Extensive network, lowest prices, watch for fees (baggage, seat selection)
  • Vueling: Spanish budget airline, good network within Spain and Europe
  • easyJet: Reliable budget carrier, good service standards
  • Volotea: Spanish low-cost, good for domestic and European routes
  • Wizz Air: Eastern Europe connections, very cheap if flexible

Full-Service Airlines

  • Iberia: Spain's flag carrier, excellent connections, codeshare with American Airlines
  • Air Europa: Spanish airline, often cheaper than Iberia, good service
  • Lufthansa, Air France, KLM: European hubs, good for connecting flights

Tips for Budget Airlines

  • Book carry-on only to avoid baggage fees (€15-60 per bag)
  • Check-in online (often free) vs airport check-in (€10-30)
  • Print or download boarding pass (some charge €5-10 for printing at airport)
  • Bring your own food (onboard food can be €5-10)
  • Measure your bag carefully (strict size limits, fees for oversized)

📅 Best Time to Book

  • International flights: Book 6-8 weeks in advance for best prices
  • European flights: Book 4-6 weeks ahead, but last-minute deals can appear
  • Domestic flights: Often cheaper than train for long distances, book 2-4 weeks ahead
  • Holiday periods: Book 3-4 months in advance (Easter, summer, Christmas)
  • Last-minute: Can find deals 1-2 weeks before, but risky for peak season

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a visa to fly to Spain?

EU/EEA citizens: No visa needed. US/Canada/UK citizens: No visa for stays under 90 days. Check requirements for your nationality.

How early should I arrive at the airport?

International flights: 3 hours. European flights: 2 hours. Domestic: 1.5 hours. Budget airlines recommend earlier.

Can I bring liquids in carry-on?

Liquids must be in containers ≤100ml, in a clear resealable bag (max 1 liter total). Purchased after security is OK.

Is it cheaper to fly into Madrid or Barcelona?

Depends on your origin and dates. Compare both - sometimes Barcelona is cheaper, sometimes Madrid. Then check train costs to your final destination.

Should I book round-trip or one-way tickets?

Usually round-trip is cheaper, but compare prices. Two separate one-way tickets sometimes offer flexibility and savings.
