🎉 El Rocío Pilgrimage

El Rocío is one of Spain’s most iconic and traditional pilgrimages, held annually in Andalusia. The festival is a powerful mix of religious devotion, folkloric celebration, and regional identity.

Every year, usually around late May or early June (coinciding with Pentecost), hundreds of thousands of pilgrims travel from cities and villages across Spain — particularly from Seville and Huelva — to the small village of El Rocío in Almonte.

Pilgrims journey by foot, on horseback, or in beautifully decorated wagons (carretas), forming colorful processions that last several days. They sing, dance, wear traditional Andalusian attire, and sleep in tents or camps along the way.

The highlight of the event is the procession of the statue of the Virgin of El Rocío, known as "La Blanca Paloma" ("The White Dove"), which takes place on Sunday night or Monday morning. It's a moving and emotional moment for many locals and visitors.
