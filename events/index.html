🎉 Cultural Events in Spain

Spain is home to some of the most unique and vibrant cultural festivals in the world. Explore traditional celebrations full of music, color, and centuries-old traditions.

El Rocío Pilgrimage

A massive Andalusian pilgrimage blending devotion, flamenco, and countryside tradition. Held every year around Pentecost near Huelva.

Feria de Abril

A week-long celebration of Andalusian culture in Seville with music, flamenco, food, and horse parades. Held every spring.
