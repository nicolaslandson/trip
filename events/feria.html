🎡 Feria de Abril (Seville Fair)

The Feria de Abril is one of the most colorful and lively festivals in Spain. Held each spring in Seville, it celebrates Andalusian culture with a week of dancing, music, traditional food, and horse parades.

The fairgrounds are filled with temporary tents called casetas, where locals gather to eat, drink, and dance sevillanas — a traditional flamenco-style dance. During the day, visitors can watch beautiful horse-drawn carriages and riders in traditional dress.

At night, the entire fair lights up with music and celebration. It's one of the best times to experience authentic local culture in Seville, and visitors are welcome to join the party.

The fair usually takes place two weeks after Easter. Book your accommodation early, as Seville gets very busy during this time.
