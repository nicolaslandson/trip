About Spain

Spain is one of Europe's most popular destinations, offering vibrant cities, stunning architecture, world-class cuisine, and beautiful coastlines. From the art museums of Madrid to Gaudí's masterpieces in Barcelona, from flamenco in Seville to the Alhambra in Granada, Spain combines rich history with modern energy. The country's diverse regions each offer unique experiences, making it perfect for first-time visitors and return travelers alike.

💰 Spain Budget Per Day

Daily travel costs in Spain vary by city and travel style:

  • Budget: €40–€60/day (hostels, markets, public transport)
  • Mid-range: €70–€120/day (hotels, restaurants, some attractions)
  • Comfort: €150+/day (boutique hotels, fine dining, guided tours)

Best Cities for First-Time Visitors

Start with Madrid or Barcelona (3–4 days each). Both offer excellent transport, English-friendly services, and major attractions. Then add Seville (2–3 days) for authentic Andalusian culture. Total: 7–10 days, €600–€1,000 per person.

🎉 Popular Festivals

  • Fallas de Valencia (March) — Spectacular festival of fire and fireworks. Valencia guide
  • Semana Santa (March/April) — Grand processions in Seville, Málaga, and other cities. Dates vary by Easter.
  • Feria de Abril (April/May) — Seville's famous spring fair with flamenco, horses, and traditional costumes.
  • San Fermín (July) — Running of the Bulls in Pamplona. Thrilling and historic festival.
  • La Tomatina (August) — The world's biggest tomato fight in Buñol, near Valencia.
  • El Rocío Pilgrimage (May/June) — Major religious event in Andalusia, usually around Pentecost.

📝 Travel Tips

  • Book AVE trains 6–10 weeks ahead for best prices (up to 70% discount). Last-minute tickets are expensive.
  • Dinner after 9pm — Spanish restaurants serve dinner late. Kitchens typically open 8:30–9pm.
  • Pickpockets: Be extra cautious on La Rambla (Barcelona), Puerta del Sol (Madrid), and crowded metros. Keep valuables secure.
  • Museums free days: Many offer free entry on certain days (often Sunday afternoons or first Sunday of month). Check websites.
  • Tipping: Not mandatory. Round up or leave €1–2 for good service. 5–10% only for exceptional service.
  • Menú del día: Best budget lunch option (€10–15 for 3 courses + drink). Available 1:30–4pm at most restaurants.
  • Learn basic Spanish: "Hola", "Gracias", "Por favor", "La cuenta" (the bill) — locals appreciate the effort.
